Privacy Policy

DocuAI

Effective 16 August 2026 Developer Devzey Package com.sidrahayat.docuai

In short

DocuAI does not collect, transmit, or share your documents. Your pages, the text read from them, and everything you type stay on your phone.

There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics or crash reporting of ours, and no cloud or AI service. We cannot see your documents, because they never reach us.

1What this policy covers

This policy explains how the DocuAI Android application handles information. It applies to the app published by Devzey under the package name com.sidrahayat.docuai.

DocuAI is an offline document scanner. It scans pages with your camera, reads the text on them, and lets you search, edit, export and ask questions about your own documents — all on the device itself.

2Information we collect

None. DocuAI has no servers, no accounts, and no network code of its own. The app does not send your documents, page images, recognised text, titles, tags, searches, or Assistant conversations anywhere.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or usage statistics.

One qualification, stated plainly in section 6: Google's ML Kit libraries inside the app include Google's own diagnostics component, which can report information about ML Kit's operation to Google. That is Google's component and it does not carry your documents.

3What the app stores, and where

Everything DocuAI creates is written to private storage on your device that only DocuAI can read:

  • Page images and inline pictures — scanned, imported, or added to a written document.
  • Recognised text — the words read from your pages by on-device text recognition.
  • Document details — titles, dates, tags, page order, and favourites.
  • Search index — built from your own documents so search works without a connection.
  • Assistant conversations — the questions you ask and the answers drawn from your pages.
  • Preferences — such as your theme and reading text size.
  • Exported files — PDFs and Word files you create, until you delete them.

Android protects this storage from other applications.

4Backup is switched off

Android's Auto Backup would normally copy an app's data to your Google Drive. DocuAI opts out explicitly, and it opts out of both transports:

  • Cloud backup — your documents and recognised text are never copied to Google Drive.
  • Device-to-device transfer — they are not carried across when you set up a new phone either. That transfer never touches Google's servers, but a document scanner holding passports and bank statements should move only when you deliberately export it.
Because nothing is backed up, your documents exist only on this device. Uninstalling DocuAI, clearing its storage, or resetting the phone deletes them permanently, and they cannot be recovered. Export anything you need to keep, and move documents to a new phone by exporting and sharing them yourself.

5Permissions and device access

DocuAI requests no runtime permissions. It never asks you to grant anything. Camera and photo access happen through Android's own system screens, which hand the app only the single item you choose.

These two permissions appear on the app's Play Store listing. They are not requested by DocuAI's own code.
Declared permissionWhere it comes from
INTERNET Added automatically by Google's ML Kit and Play Services libraries, which use it for the diagnostics described in section 6. No DocuAI feature makes network requests.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Added by the same libraries, so they can tell whether a connection is available.

Camera

Scanning opens Google Play Services' document scanner, which uses the camera under its own permission and returns the captured page to DocuAI. The camera is used only while you are scanning, and the images are saved to your device.

Photos and files

Importing opens Android's system photo picker or file chooser. You pick one item, and Android grants DocuAI access to that item alone. The app cannot browse your gallery or your storage.

Not used

DocuAI does not use the microphone, location, contacts, calendar, Bluetooth, notifications, or any background sensor. It holds no camera or storage permission of its own.

6Google Play Services and ML Kit

DocuAI uses two Google components, both of which process your pages on the device:

  • Document Scanner — camera capture, edge detection and perspective correction. Delivered as a Google Play Services module, which Play Services downloads in its own process the first time you scan. Images are processed locally.
  • Text Recognition — reads the text from your pages. The model is bundled inside the app, so it works with no connection at all.

These libraries also include Google's data-transport component, which can send Google information about ML Kit's own operation — such as which features ran and whether they succeeded. This is Google's telemetry about its own software, it is the reason the app carries the INTERNET permission, and it is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Your documents, page images, recognised text and questions are not part of that. Nothing in DocuAI reads them and sends them anywhere.

DocuAI contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking libraries of its own.

7The Assistant is not a cloud AI

The DocuAI Assistant is not a chatbot connected to an AI service. It answers by searching the text already stored on your device and quoting the pages it came from. There is no language model and no external service involved, and no question you ask is sent to any AI provider.

8When information leaves your device

Only when you choose to send it. If you use Share or Export, DocuAI hands the file you selected to Android's share sheet and you choose where it goes — email, messaging, cloud storage, or a printer.

From that moment the file is in the hands of the app or service you picked, and that app's own privacy policy applies, not this one. DocuAI never shares anything without you asking.

9Sharing and selling data

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your information with anyone. There is no data to share: none of it reaches us.

10Keeping and deleting your data

Your documents stay on your device until you remove them. You are in full control:

  • Delete a single document inside the app at any time. Its pages and images are removed from storage with it.
  • Clear the app's storage from Android Settings to remove everything at once.
  • Uninstall DocuAI to remove all of its data from your device.

Because DocuAI holds no data about you on any server, there is nothing to request, correct or delete from us.

11Accounts and advertising

DocuAI has no sign-in, no user accounts, and no advertising. The app is fully usable the moment it is installed.

12Children

DocuAI is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

13Security

Your documents are stored in DocuAI's private application storage, which Android protects from other apps. Keeping your device locked with a PIN, pattern, or biometric is the most effective way to protect what is on it.

14Changes to this policy

If a future version of DocuAI changes what it stores or gains network access, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the change will be visible in the app's permission list on Google Play. Updated versions appear at this address with a new effective date.
